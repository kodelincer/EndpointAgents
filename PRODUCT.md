# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

A single technical operator monitors Windows endpoint agents, inspects connection health, and runs approved diagnostics during local development and support work.

## Product Purpose

Replace terminal-only control-plane observation with a live, inspectable dashboard. Success means an operator can see agents connect and disconnect, safely issue a catalogued command, and inspect the correlated JSON-RPC exchange without refreshing or using command-line tools.

## Positioning

An operational window into every live endpoint interaction, from connection through RPC result.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and dependable. The interface should feel like an engineering instrument rather than a marketing dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative terminal cosplay, glass dashboards, oversized metric cards, excessive rounding, and visual effects that compete with operational data.

## Design Principles

- Put live state and actionable agents first.
- Make every command reviewable before execution.
- Keep request and response evidence easy to inspect and copy.
- Explain empty, reconnecting, and failed states directly.
- Prefer familiar controls and dense, readable information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with complete keyboard operation, visible focus, non-color status cues, reduced-motion support, and sufficient contrast.
